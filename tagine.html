Chicken Tagine

Chicken slow-braised with saffron, preserved lemons and olives in Morocco's iconic clay pot.

  • Total time1 h 30 min
  • Serves4
  • DifficultyMedium
Start cooking

Ingredients

4
  • 8 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 2 large onions, grated
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 large pinch saffron threads
  • 2 preserved lemons, rind sliced
  • 1 cup green olives
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 1 handful fresh coriander and parsley
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

About this dish

A tagine is both a dish and the conical clay pot it's cooked in. The tall lid traps rising steam, which condenses and drips back down, gently braising meat and vegetables in their own juices with very little water, a clever technique developed by the Amazigh (Berber) people of North Africa.

This classic Moroccan version pairs chicken with tangy preserved lemons, green olives and a golden blend of ginger, turmeric, cumin and precious saffron. It's traditionally eaten straight from the pot, scooped up with pieces of fresh khobz bread.

Region: North Africa

Cooking instructions

Tap each stage to expand or collapse the steps.

01Marinate the chicken
  1. Soak the saffron in 2 tablespoons of warm water for 10 minutes.
  2. Mix the garlic, olive oil, ginger, turmeric, cumin, paprika, salt and pepper into a paste.
  3. Rub the paste all over the chicken and leave to marinate for at least 1 hour (or overnight).
02Brown and braise
  1. Warm the tagine or a heavy casserole over medium heat and brown the chicken on both sides.
  2. Add the grated onion, saffron water, cinnamon stick and stock, and stir gently.
  3. Cover and simmer on low heat for 45 minutes, turning the chicken halfway.
03Finish with lemon and olives
  1. Scatter the preserved lemon rind and olives around the chicken.
  2. Cover and cook for a final 15 minutes until the sauce is thick and glossy.
  3. Garnish with chopped herbs and serve with khobz bread or fluffy couscous.

Chef's tip: Using a clay tagine on a gas or electric hob? Place a heat diffuser underneath and always heat it slowly to stop it cracking.
